
#### Integrations

##### New: Recorded Future Intelligence

New: Get quick reputation and risk context for IPs, domains, URLs, files, and CVEs from Recorded Future using the ***ip***, ***domain***, ***url***, ***file*** and ***cve*** commands. Each command returns the Recorded Future risk score, triggered risk rules and evidence, and sets the DBot score and indicator context. Files, URLs and domains with no observed risk are scored as *Good*, and the reason for each score is written to *DBotScore.Message*. (Available from Cortex XSOAR 6.0.0).
